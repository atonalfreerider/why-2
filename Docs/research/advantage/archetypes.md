# Competitive advantage before the corporation, and in nature

A recursive reading of the archetypes: what each winner stood on, whom it beat, and what restored the balance. Every number is cited by fact id.

## The Mongols: a system, not a stirrup

The stirrup was not Mongol. It was invented in China in the 4th century and spread across Eurasia through nomadic peoples [archetypes-stirrup-chinese-origin]. The stronger version of the point: the Mongol advantage was a system. Each rider kept three or four horses and rotated mounts [archetypes-mongol-horses-per-soldier]; the composite bow was small enough for the saddle and quivers of 60 arrows were strapped to rider and horse [archetypes-mongol-bow-quivers]. Genghis rebuilt a tribal society into an integrated meritocracy [archetypes-mongol-meritocracy] and had conquered all Mongol and Turkic tribes by 1206 [archetypes-mongol-unite-tribes-1206].

The absorption of the defeated is the part the user stressed, and the sources back it: prisoners were driven forward in sieges [archetypes-mongol-prisoners-driven-forward]; one thousand North Chinese engineer squads accompanied Hulagu [archetypes-mongol-chinese-engineers]; in 1273 two Muslim engineers built counterweight trebuchets [archetypes-mongol-muslim-engineers-1273]. By 1227 the empire was twice the size of the Roman Empire, the largest contiguous state in history [archetypes-mongol-empire-extent].

Triad: information (couriers, captured engineers), execution (horses, bow, meritocracy), environment (the steppe). Desire: the whole settled world. Fear: succession splits among the khan's heirs. Balance: the empire divided into khanates rather than being beaten by one coalition.

## Alexander: the line and the wedge

His phalanx with long pikes outclassed Persian scimitars and javelins [archetypes-alexander-phalanx-edge]. At Gaugamela, heavily outnumbered, the Macedonians won through tactics and light infantry [archetypes-gaugamela-outnumbered]; Alexander led a wedge into the weakened centre [archetypes-gaugamela-wedge]. Darius's only respectable infantry were 2,000 Greek hoplites and the 10,000 Immortals [archetypes-darius-immortals]. Afterwards Alexander gave Persians command posts and Macedonian titles for Persian units [archetypes-alexander-persian-officers], the same absorption pattern as the Mongols, at the cost of Macedonian resentment. The restoring force was internal: exhausted troops and a divided succession.

## Napoleon: mass, corps, artillery, and a crown taken

The levy of 1793 (about 300,000 men) [archetypes-napoleon-levy-1793], eventually 750,000 to 800,000 conscripts [archetypes-napoleon-levy-conscripts] filled the ranks. The Grande Armée had six corps under marshals [archetypes-napoleon-grande-armee-corps], and corps marched apart and fought together to win at Jena and Auerstedt on 14 October 1806 [archetypes-napoleon-jena-1806]. After 1807 massed artillery was the spearhead [archetypes-napoleon-massed-artillery]. In 1814, 30,000 men inflicted 20,000 casualties for 2,000 lost [archetypes-napoleon-six-days-1814].

On the political side, the Senate vested government in an emperor on 18 May 1804 [archetypes-napoleon-emperor-1804], and Napoleon crowned himself rather than receive the crown from the Church [archetypes-napoleon-crowned-himself]. The user's phrase "monarchy to tyranny" is looser than the record: the Revolution ended divine-right rule in France, and Napoleon then took a crown himself.

Balance: the Sixth Coalition won at Leipzig in October 1813 [archetypes-napoleon-leipzig-1813], the Seventh at Waterloo in June 1815 [archetypes-napoleon-waterloo-1815]. The Treaty of Chaumont was the cornerstone of the alliance [archetypes-vienna-chaumont]; the Congress sat from September 1814 to June 1815 [archetypes-vienna-congress-dates], and the Concert of Europe kept a balance until 1914 [archetypes-vienna-concert-of-europe].

## The World Wars: weight against overreach

German steel passed Britain's in 1893 [archetypes-ww1-german-steel-1893]. In 1914 the Schlieffen Plan put 80% of the army in the West [archetypes-ww1-schlieffen-80], a bet on speed against two fronts. The United States joined in April 1917 after unrestricted submarine warfare [archetypes-ww1-us-joined-1917]. Germany's WWII production depended on resources outside its control [archetypes-ww2-germany-resource-dependence], and the US entered in December 1941 [archetypes-ww2-us-enters-1941].

The industrial ledger: Allied crude oil 1,043,000,000 tonnes against 66,000,000 [archetypes-ww2-crude-oil], coal 4,581,400,000 against 2,629,900,000 [archetypes-ww2-coal-allies-axis]. American industry made nearly two thirds of Allied munitions [archetypes-ww2-us-two-thirds-munitions]; Roosevelt called for 185,000 aeroplanes [archetypes-ww2-roosevelt-185000-aircraft] when 1939 output was under 3,000 [archetypes-ww2-hitler-propaganda]. Lend-Lease shipped $50.1 billion [archetypes-lendlease-total], $31.4 billion to the UK and $11.3 billion to the USSR [archetypes-lendlease-uk].

The user's premise holds in outline: Germany was the strongest Continental industrial economy and pushed itself out of balance, and the coalitions had the larger resource base. Two caveats: overall German-versus-British industrial output before 1914 is debated (steel is clear), and the postwar US gain was a 27.3% share of world economic activity in 1950 with 6.0% of population and 66.3% of military spending [archetypes-us-1950-share-of-activity]. Bretton Woods set rules for 44 countries [archetypes-bretton-woods-44] on a base of two thirds of the world's gold held by the US [archetypes-bretton-woods-gold].

## The biosphere

Only about 10% of energy passes up each trophic level [archetypes-trophic-ten-percent], so predators are few and depend on a broad prey base. Predator and prey regulate each other [archetypes-lotka-volterra]; lynx rise and fall with snowshoe hares [archetypes-lynx-hare-cycle]. The end-Cretaceous extinction killed about 75% of species [archetypes-kpg-three-quarters]; non-avian dinosaurs could not shelter [archetypes-kpg-dinosaurs-no-shelter] while all major mammal lineages survived with losses [archetypes-kpg-mammals-survived]. Environment shapes organisms: flight evolved independently in insects, birds, pterosaurs and bats [archetypes-convergent-flight].

## What a strategist would predict

1. Advantages built as systems last longer than single technologies; the Mongols won with a bundle, not a stirrup.
2. Absorbing the defeated's skills compounds advantage, but strains loyalty.
3. A power that gets ahead of its neighbours triggers a coalition (Napoleon, Germany); the larger resource base wins a long war.
4. A top predator is exposed when its base shrinks: the lesson for any firm at a choke point living on a few customers.
5. Shocks favour the flexible: the mammal lineages, not the dinosaurs.
