## What "the sweep" is and what it tracked (the answer to the question)

The sweep is the economy scene's Wave view (preset `overview`: "The US economy, a wave through time"): a terrain of industry
ridges with one rib a year, the crest at the year shown and the past behind it, and companies as gold lines rising with
market value. On 8 Nov 2026 it tracked 75 hand-picked companies (`Assets/Resources/Data/economy/company-lives.json`) across
15 of the 23 market industries: not only the tech supply chain (manufacturing 18, hardware 10, retail 8, banking 7,
software 6, internet 4, media and telecom 4, finance 4, insurance 3, hospitality 3, oil and gas 2, mining and metals 2,
transport 2, utilities 1, entertainment 1), but chosen for famous rises and falls, with none in agriculture, construction,
real estate, health care, legal, professional services, education or other services, and their market values recalled
approximations (+-30-50%), not measurements. The advantage view (the corporate biosphere) and the history view's AI story
do track the AI and tech supply chain.
