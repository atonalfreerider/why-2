# Economy beyond the AI chain, and its environment (7 November 2026)

## The scoreboard

On the CompaniesMarketCap table of 7 November 2026 the twenty-five largest companies are, in order: Apple $4.975 trillion [economy-mcap-apple], NVIDIA $4.506 T [economy-mcap-nvidia], Microsoft $3.940 T [economy-mcap-microsoft], Alphabet $3.490 T [economy-mcap-google], Amazon $2.803 T [economy-mcap-amazon], TSMC $1.947 T [economy-mcap-tsmc], SpaceX $1.823 T [economy-mcap-spacex], Saudi Aramco $1.687 T [economy-mcap-saudi-aramco], Meta $1.560 T [economy-mcap-meta], Tesla $1.415 T [economy-mcap-tesla], Broadcom $1.401 T [economy-mcap-broadcom], Berkshire Hathaway $1.222 T [economy-mcap-berkshire], Eli Lilly $1.206 T [economy-mcap-eli-lilly], Walmart $995.84 B [economy-mcap-walmart], Samsung $927.27 B [economy-mcap-samsung], JPMorgan $888.27 B [economy-mcap-jpmorgan], Visa $789.93 B [economy-mcap-visa], Micron $780.68 B [economy-mcap-micron], AMD $736.46 B [economy-mcap-amd], Exxon Mobil $676.62 B [economy-mcap-exxon-mobil], Johnson & Johnson $664.94 B [economy-mcap-johnson-and-johnson], SK Hynix $602.37 B [economy-mcap-sk-hynix], Mastercard $568.61 B [economy-mcap-mastercard], ASML $549.12 B [economy-mcap-asml], AbbVie $514.92 B [economy-mcap-abbvie].

Read it as a map of advantages. Fourteen of the twenty-five (Apple, NVIDIA, Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon, TSMC, SpaceX with xAI inside it, Meta, Broadcom, Samsung, Micron, AMD, SK Hynix, ASML) sit in or on the AI chain. The rest are the economy it is built on: oil (Aramco, Exxon), a car-and-robot bet (Tesla), a patient holding company (Berkshire), pharma (Lilly, J&J, AbbVie), retail (Walmart), and the toll-roads of money (JPMorgan, Visa, Mastercard). The pattern: scarcity of something others must buy (a molecule, a lithography machine, a sea lane, a payment rail, a customer base) is rewarded far above ordinary profit.

## The environment of 2026

- **Concentration.** The ten largest members were 39.1% of the S&P 500 at 30 September 2026 and the largest alone 8.4% [economy-sp500-top10-weight]. The market is a bet on a handful of names.
- **AI is the growth.** ING estimates that broad tech investment (data centres, information processing, software, before subtracting imports) was 50.2% of year-on-year US GDP growth in Q2 2026, and averaged 46.6% over four quarters [economy-ai-share-of-gdp-growth]. ING's own view is that AI is about one-third of growth; the gross figure overstates the domestic gain because much of the hardware is imported.
- **Where the money comes from.** The four hyperscalers plan $725 billion of capex in 2026, up 77% from $410 billion [economy-hyperscaler-capex-2026]. US venture investment hit $515.8 billion through Q3, about 44% above the 2021 record [economy-vc-deal-value-q3], and AI took 82.7% of it [economy-vc-ai-share]. The openness the user describes (risk capital, new firms) is working, but it is working on one theme. New-business applications remain high: 534,947 in September 2026 [economy-business-applications-sep2026].
- **Price of money.** The Fed held at 3-3/4 to 4 percent on 28 October 2026 [economy-fed-hold-oct2026]; two members voted to raise [economy-fed-dissent-hike] and the statement says inflation remains elevated [economy-fed-inflation-statement]. The cycle is not one of cuts.
- **Tariffs.** The Supreme Court struck down the IEEPA tariffs; the Penn Wharton Budget Model projected up to $175 billion in refunds [economy-tariff-refunds-wharton]. Apple's gross margin included about a point from refunds [economy-apple-q4-gross-margin]. Importers who could absorb the tariffs (scale) are now being repaid.
- **Energy and war.** The US-Israeli war on Iran disrupted the Strait of Hormuz, which carried about 20% of global oil before the war [economy-hormuz-oil-share]; the EIA expects Brent to average about $98 in 2026 [economy-eia-brent-2026]. This is the environment variable that most differs from the 2025 picture.

## How the rest of the economy connects to the AI chain

Three roles. **Who buys AI:** consumers through Apple's devices, enterprises through the clouds, and Tencent's WeChat in China (it said it substantially stepped up its procurement of compute [economy-tencent-compute-statement]). **Who powers it:** oil and gas set the price of energy, and the Gulf holds the sea lane; Aramco's adjusted net income rose to $31.2 billion in Q3 [economy-aramco-q3-adjusted-ni]. **Who finances it:** venture capital (82.7% AI), the public markets (39.1% in ten names), banks such as JPMorgan (Q3 net income $15.3 billion [economy-jpm-q3-net-income]) and the Fed's price of money. Tesla holds SpaceX equity [economy-tesla-spacex-stake], and SpaceX now contains an AI lab, so the car company, the rocket company and the AI chain are tied by ownership.

## The leaders, their advantage, fear and flashpoints

**Apple.** Advantage: switching costs and brand on an installed base. Q4 revenue $114.5 billion, up 12% [economy-apple-q4-revenue]; its CEO calls it a September-quarter record and its best fiscal year ever [economy-apple-ceo-statement]. Desire: keep the Services flywheel. Fear: the AI interface moving to someone else's model; tariffs returning. Flashpoint: the tariff refunds (fp-apple-vs-tariffs).

**Berkshire Hathaway.** Advantage: cheap, sticky capital and patience. Cash was $365.5 billion at 30 June, down 8.0% from a record $397.4 billion [economy-berkshire-cash-q2]; the new CEO began to deploy it. Fear: a permanent loss of capital, or a market in which nothing is cheap enough (fp-berkshire-cash).

**JPMorgan.** Advantage: balance-sheet scale and the deposit base. It earned $15.3 billion in Q3 [economy-jpm-q3-net-income]. Fear: a credit event in the loans that fund the AI build.

**Eli Lilly against Novo Nordisk.** Lilly's Q3 revenue was $24.40 billion, up 39% [economy-lilly-q3-revenue], and guidance rose to $91-$92 billion [economy-lilly-guidance-2026]; Novo guides 2026 adjusted sales to -2% to +2% [economy-novo-outlook-2026] while claiming more than 50% of US branded obesity new starts [economy-novo-wegovy-share] and Q3 net sales of DKK 79.2 billion [economy-novo-q3-net-sales]. The mechanism: a cornered resource (the patent) turned into process power (supply). Novo made the market; Lilly took the growth (fp-glp1-lilly-vs-novo). A strategist predicts the leader keeps share as long as its drug works better and payers do not force price down faster than volume rises.

**Tesla against BYD.** In Q3 2026 BYD sold 762,478 passenger battery-electric cars against Tesla's 486,532 [economy-tesla-vs-byd-q3-deliveries]. Tesla's operating margin was 2.6% against 5.8% a year earlier [economy-tesla-q3-operating-margin] while capex doubled to $4.73 billion [economy-tesla-q3-capex]. BYD's advantage is process power and integration; Tesla's response is to move the bet to robotaxis and robots. Yet the market still values Tesla at $1.415 trillion [economy-mcap-tesla]. The prediction: cars are a commodity in which BYD wins on cost; Tesla's value is now an option on autonomy (fp-byd-vs-tesla).

**SpaceX.** It listed on 12 June 2026 at $135 and closed near $161, valuing it above $2 trillion [economy-spacex-ipo], and ranks seventh at $1.823 trillion [economy-mcap-spacex]. Advantage: the lowest cost to orbit, plus capital (fp-spacex-ipo). Fear: an IPO valuation that assumes growth it cannot deliver.

**Saudi Aramco.** Cornered resource at the sea lane. Q3 adjusted net income $31.2 billion [economy-aramco-q3-adjusted-ni], downstream adjusted EBIT up 234.0% [economy-aramco-downstream], a $21.9 billion quarterly base dividend [economy-aramco-q3-dividend], and profit up despite attacks on its facilities [economy-aramco-attacks]. Fear: Hormuz closure and demand destruction (fp-hormuz-aramco).

**Walmart.** Scale economies plus advertising: global advertising grew 38% and Walmart Connect in the US 43% excluding VIZIO [economy-walmart-advertising-q2]. Fear: tariffs and a consumer who trades down.

**Visa (and Mastercard).** A network toll: fiscal 2026 net revenue $45.9 billion, up 15% [economy-visa-fy2026-net-revenue]. A choke point for consumption rather than AI.

**Tencent.** Network economies in WeChat; Q2 2026 revenue 204.8 billion yuan (US$30.36 billion), with net profit up only 0.7 percent as it spends on compute [economy-tencent-q2-revenue]. Fear: chip supply.

## Choke points

The Strait of Hormuz [economy-hormuz-oil-share]; the Fed's price of money [economy-fed-hold-oct2026]; the concentration of public equity in ten names [economy-sp500-top10-weight]; tirzepatide at Lilly [economy-lilly-q3-revenue]; the card rail at Visa [economy-visa-fy2026-net-revenue]. The capital rivers: venture money to AI start-ups ($426.6 billion in nine months, 82.7% of $515.8 billion) and hyperscaler capex ($725 billion) both end in the AI chain; the tariff refund river ($175 billion projected) runs from government back to importers; Aramco's dividend (about $87.6 billion annualised) flows to the Gulf.

## What a strategist would predict

1. **Friendly scenario** (rates steady, oil easing, AI revenue arrives): the concentrated top rises further; Apple, Microsoft and Alphabet keep altitude because they own customers; Lilly and Visa keep their tolls.
2. **Unfriendly scenario** (Fed hikes as two members wanted, oil stays near $98, AI revenue disappoints): the weakest links are the firms whose value rests on an option (Tesla, SpaceX) and the capex-financed clouds; Berkshire, Aramco and Visa hold up (cash, scarcity, toll). The ten-name index share (39.1%) and the 82.7% AI share of venture money mean the fall would be correlated, not diversified.
3. **A cheaper-model or demand-pull-back shock** hits the chain first, then consumers and credit through JPMorgan-type balance sheets; the toll-road firms (Visa) are least exposed.
4. **Tail:** Hormuz. The gulf is the one choke point where a single event changes energy costs for everyone including data centres.

Dominance is not permanent: Novo's story (the category creator overtaken by a better-supplied rival) and Tesla's (a first mover overtaken on cost) show the path every top-25 name faces if its advantage is copied faster than it can be protected.
