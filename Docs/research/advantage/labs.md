# Labs, clouds, energy, capital and talent: the 2026 frontier

Today is 7 November 2026. This is the recursive analysis of the model labs and the money, power and people that stand under them. Numbers in brackets are verified fact ids in `labs.json`.

## The shape of the contest

Three labs sit on top: OpenAI (valuation $852bn [labs-openai-valuation], run-rate roughly $50bn on 8 October [labs-openai-run-rate]), Anthropic ($965bn after its May Series H [labs-anthropic-valuation], run-rate past $47bn [labs-anthropic-run-rate-may], up from about $9bn at the end of 2025 [labs-anthropic-run-rate-apr]) and xAI, now inside SpaceX at a combined $1.25tn [labs-xai-spacex-merger-value]. Anthropic's reported 2025 revenue was $4.585bn [labs-anthropic-revenue-2025], so run-rate and booked revenue are very different yardsticks; both labs also lose money (xAI's segment lost $6.4bn on $3.2bn revenue in 2025 [labs-xai-segment-2025]).

Underneath are the clouds, who are really the winners of the spending: 2026 capex is guided at about $220bn for Amazon [labs-amazon-capex-2026], $195-205bn for Alphabet [labs-alphabet-capex-2026], around $170bn for Microsoft in its fiscal year [labs-microsoft-capex-fy27], $135-145bn for Meta [labs-meta-capex-2026] and $70bn of Oracle's own money in fiscal 2027 [labs-oracle-capex-fy27]. Alphabet's third-quarter capex of $51.4bn exceeded its $42.3bn operating cash flow [labs-google-cloud-q3]. Cloud is paying back for Alphabet, where Cloud revenue doubled to $30.7bn [labs-google-cloud-q3].

## Advantages, named

- **OpenAI**: branding and scale (900 million weekly users [labs-openai-weekly-users]); the advantage is bought with commitments. Contracts: Oracle $300bn over five years [labs-openai-oracle-300bn], Azure an incremental $250bn [labs-microsoft-openai-stake], AWS $38bn over seven years [labs-openai-aws-38bn], CoreWeave $22.4bn [labs-openai-coreweave-22bn], NVIDIA up to $100bn of equity as gigawatts deploy [labs-openai-nvidia-100bn], AMD 6 GW with a warrant for 160m shares [labs-openai-amd-warrant], Broadcom 10 GW [labs-openai-broadcom-10gw]. SoftBank has put in $64.6bn [labs-softbank-openai-total].
- **Anthropic**: a cornered resource in talent (seven OpenAI veterans founded it [labs-anthropic-founding]) and process power in selling to enterprises. It buys compute from everyone: $30bn of Azure with NVIDIA and Microsoft investing up to $10bn and $5bn [labs-anthropic-azure-nvidia], up to one million Google TPUs [labs-anthropic-google-tpu], 3.5 GW through Broadcom from 2027 [labs-anthropic-broadcom-tpu], $50bn of its own sites [labs-anthropic-fluidstack-50bn] and $1.25bn a month to rent xAI's Colossus [labs-anthropic-colossus-contract].
- **Microsoft**: holds about 27 percent of OpenAI [labs-microsoft-openai-stake] and sells Claude too.
- **Alphabet**: the only full stack (chips, cloud, models); it earns when Anthropic buys TPUs.
- **Oracle**: $664bn of backlog [labs-oracle-rpo] but $28.5bn capex in one quarter [labs-oracle-capex-q1]; it carries the cost of its customers' promise.
- **Power**: IEA projects 945 TWh for data centres in 2030 [labs-iea-datacentre-2030]; GE Vernova's gas backlog and slot reservations reached 122 GW, heading to at least 130 [labs-gev-gas-backlog].

## Fear and desire, in their own words

Zuckerberg: "If we end up misspending a couple hundred billion dollars, that's going to be very unfortunate obviously. But I would say the risk is higher on the other side" [labs-zuckerberg-misspend]: fear of being late outweighs fear of waste. Amodei states the other fear: with revenue at $800bn instead of $1tn, "no hedge on Earth" would stop bankruptcy if he bought that much compute [labs-amodei-bankrupt]. Pichai: "No company is going to be immune, including us." [labs-pichai-irrationality]. Meta's talent offers, reported up to $300m over four years [labs-meta-talent-pay], show people priced like assets.

## Flashpoints and choke points

Chips-for-equity (NVIDIA, AMD) makes suppliers finance demand: a circular loop. OpenAI's escape from Microsoft exclusivity let Oracle, AWS and CoreWeave in. Anthropic's hedged compute plan is the counter to OpenAI's commitment-heavy plan. Choke points: gas turbines (GE Vernova), Oracle as holder of OpenAI's biggest contract, Microsoft's stake.

## What a strategist would predict

If the growth curves hold (Anthropic's run-rate more than quintupled in five months), the clouds win in every scenario and the labs split by who matched commitments to revenue. If growth stalls by a year, the fragile parties are those who hold the cost of other people's promises: Oracle, CoreWeave, SoftBank, and OpenAI. Anthropic's hedge protects it, and Alphabet and Amazon are paid either way. Unfriendly environment: power shortage (benefits GE Vernova, hurts everyone's timelines), export controls, a cheaper model, higher rates. NVIDIA is exposed to its own customers' credit because it finances them.
