# Owners: the golden circles of 2026

Written 7 Nov 2026. Cluster `owners`: who owns the giants, and what that ownership lets them do. 77 facts, all through
the truth gate. Ids in brackets.

## The owners in four layers

**1. The index layer owns everything, and owns it quietly.** BlackRock, Vanguard and State Street each hold a large
slice of every US giant. At NVIDIA: BlackRock 8.06% [owners-blackrock-nvidia-stake], Vanguard Capital Management 6.39%
[owners-vanguard-nvidia-stake], State Street 4.19% [owners-state-street-nvidia-stake]: about 18.6% together. Microsoft
(8.18%, 6.53%, 4.25%) [owners-blackrock-microsoft-stake, owners-vanguard-microsoft-stake,
owners-state-street-microsoft-stake] and Apple (8.00%, 6.60%, 4.23%) [owners-blackrock-apple-stake,
owners-vanguard-apple-stake, owners-state-street-apple-stake] look the same: the three hold roughly 19% of each. Yahoo
splits Vanguard into legal entities, so its true total is higher (Apple adds 2.30% in Portfolio Management; see notes in
the facts). Fidelity (FMR) follows with 4.26% of NVIDIA [owners-fidelity-nvidia-stake] and 4.26% of Alphabet
[owners-fidelity-google-stake]. Berkshire itself is held 8.41% by Vanguard [owners-vanguard-berkshire-stake] and 1.04% by
the Gates Foundation Trust [owners-gates-foundation-trust-berkshire-stake]. These holders do not choose winners; they own
the whole index, so they hold every contestant and vote on all of them. For the simulation they are the gold ring
that surrounds the whole field, and they are the slow channel of household savings into the giants.

**2. Founders keep control with less than half the equity.** The strategic fact of 2026 is the dual-class share.
Zuckerberg controls about 61% of Meta's votes on about 13% of the shares [owners-zuckerberg-meta-voting,
owners-zuckerberg-meta-economic]. Page and Brin together hold 52.7% of Alphabet's votes [owners-page-brin-alphabet-voting].
Musk controls more than 82% of SpaceX's votes [owners-musk-spacex-voting], a stake worth roughly $866 billion at the IPO
price [owners-musk-spacex-value], and about 19.9% of Tesla after exercising his 2018 options in June 2026
[owners-musk-tesla-stake]. Michael Dell has an economic stake reported at 40% to 47% [owners-dell-michael-economic] and
affiliated holders carry roughly 93% of Dell's votes [owners-dell-affiliates-voting]. Ellison holds roughly 40% of Oracle
[owners-ellison-oracle-stake], Bezos about 8.8% of Amazon [owners-bezos-amazon-stake], Huang about 3.5% of NVIDIA
[owners-huang-nvidia-stake], Ballmer an estimated 4% of Microsoft [owners-ballmer-microsoft-stake]. Buffett controls 30.2%
of Berkshire's votes and is giving the shares away [owners-buffett-berkshire-voting]. A founder with a vote that outruns
his equity can run a long, loss-making campaign for market share that a pure shareholder would not tolerate: the stock
price funds the war, and the public cannot stop it.

**3. Companies own each other, and the circle closes.** NVIDIA is also an owner: $30 billion into OpenAI
[owners-nvidia-openai-30bn], a $5 billion Intel bet worth more than $25 billion now [owners-nvidia-intel-5bn-stake],
10.29% of CoreWeave [owners-nvidia-coreweave-stake] after $2 billion more [owners-nvidia-coreweave-2bn], and 1.49% of
SpaceX [owners-nvidia-spacex-stake]. The supplier owns its customers, so demand for GPUs and the owner's stake rise
together. Microsoft holds about 27% of OpenAI, the OpenAI Foundation 26%, and investors and employees 47%
[owners-microsoft-openai-27, owners-openai-foundation-openai-stake]. OpenAI's 2026 round raised $122 billion at $852
billion, co-led by SoftBank with Amazon, Nvidia and Microsoft, and a16z, D. E. Shaw, MGX, TPG and T. Rowe Price accounts
[owners-openai-round-2026-leads]: Amazon committed $50 billion, $15 billion first [owners-amazon-openai-50bn], SoftBank
and Nvidia $30 billion each [owners-softbank-openai-round-30bn]. SoftBank's stake is about 13% [owners-softbank-openai-stake].
Anthropic took money from both clouds but capped them: Amazon at 12%, Google at 14.99%
[owners-amazon-anthropic-cap, owners-google-anthropic-cap], after $8.0 billion of Amazon notes
[owners-amazon-anthropic-notes-8bn] and up to $40 billion from Google [owners-google-anthropic-40bn]; GIC and Coatue led
the $30 billion Series G [owners-gic-coatue-anthropic-series-g], Altimeter, Dragoneer, Greenoaks and Sequoia the $65
billion Series H [owners-series-h-leads]. Alphabet owns 6.68% of SpaceX at 30 June 2026 [owners-google-spacex-stake], against
6.11% at the end of 2025 [owners-google-spacex-2025], before the xAI merger diluted it. Valor holds 6.10% [owners-valor-spacex-stake], Vy Capital 3.30% [owners-vy-capital-spacex-stake],
Saudi Arabia's PIF 1.87% [owners-pif-spacex-stake]. Magnetar is CoreWeave's largest holder at 11.35%
[owners-magnetar-coreweave-stake]. SoftBank holds 1.64% of Intel [owners-softbank-intel-stake]; Berkshire 1.57% of Apple
[owners-berkshire-apple-stake].

**4. The households at the bottom of every circle.** US households and nonprofits held $46.0 trillion of corporate equities
at the Q3 2025 Fed count, of $108.5 trillion in all [owners-households-equities]; foreign investors held $19.6 trillion
of US equities [owners-foreign-us-equities]; mutual funds $17.0 trillion [owners-mutual-funds-equities]. The funds are the
pipe between households and the giants. In 2026 a second pipe opened: OpenAI took about $3 billion from individual
investors for the first time [owners-retail-openai-3bn]. Norway's fund is the clearest sovereign: about 1.3% of Microsoft,
Apple and Amazon [owners-nbim-microsoft-stake, owners-nbim-apple-stake, owners-nbim-amazon-stake].

## What the view should draw

- **Gold circles**, sized by the dollar value of their holdings (13F value) and tethered to each company with a thickness
  of their percentage; index managers sit in one ring above the field, founders sit above their own company.
- **A control gap.** For each controlled company, draw the vote ring larger than the equity ring (Meta 61% vs 13%).
- **Cross-holding edges** from owner companies to the companies they fund: NVIDIA to OpenAI, CoreWeave, Intel; Amazon and
  Google to Anthropic; Microsoft, SoftBank and Amazon to OpenAI. These are the circular rivers of capital.
- **Households** as the base layer feeding the funds, and a thin new direct stream to OpenAI.

## What a strategist predicts from it

1. **Index ownership makes the giants' fall a household event.** Nineteen per cent of NVIDIA, Microsoft and Apple sits in
   three index complexes, so a collapse in the AI cohort propagates straight into $46 trillion of household equity.
2. **Founder control is the real moat in a downturn.** Meta, Alphabet, SpaceX and Dell cannot be forced to retrench by
   shareholders; Microsoft, NVIDIA and Amazon can. In a bust the first group burns longer, and either wins the war of
   attrition or fails harder.
3. **Circular ownership concentrates the risk.** If OpenAI's $852 billion shell deflates, Microsoft (27%), SoftBank
   (13%, funded by notes), Amazon ($50 billion committed) and NVIDIA ($30 billion) mark down at once, while their
   revenues from OpenAI also fall.
4. **Caps are a counter-positioning move.** Anthropic's 12% and 14.99% ceilings are what lets it take two rivals' money.
